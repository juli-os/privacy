Makro — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Makro collects no personal data. The app contains no analytics, no tracking, and no advertising SDKs. The developer has no access to any of your content.

Everything you see in the app — conversations, files, agent activity, push notifications — travels directly between your device and the server address you configure (typically your own computer running the open-source juli-service engine, github.com/juli-os). Credentials you enter (server address and password) are stored only in your device's Keychain.

Voice input, if you enable it, is transcribed by your own Azure Speech Services subscription using your own API key. Voice audio is processed by that service under your account; the developer is not a party to it.

Push notifications (optional) are delivered via Apple Push Notification service from your own server to your device. Notification content originates from your server, not from the developer.

Contact: contact@juliasia.cn
